Stress Training for Dogs

Escape and avoidance training to teach Griz to handle stress better and listen even when no rewards are available. This is 4 sessions of forced fetch training.

Trailing Games, Training Through Play

Search and rescue style double blind retrieves. Two red balls (hard for dogs to see, so she has to use her nose) are placed out of sight, then Griz is cast to each one in order.

Tracking Demonstration, 12 Month Old GSD

Competition-style tracking for a dog too young to compete. A fun way to give a dog mental stimulation, and layering fetch at the end makes it a complete body and mind workout.

Trailing Training with Griz and Kids

Griz at 1 year old, showing she can find her kid if he gets lost in the woods.

Training Through Play with Griz

How we play: turns, heel tricks, parade, center, boost, and backing up.

Training Kids with Dogs

My 6 year old Norwegian Viking child walking on leash and playing with big dogs: a 1 year old working line German Shepherd and an 11 year old husky rescue. Can your six-year-old do that with a working line German Shepherd?

Off-Leash Enrichment Walk By a Lake

A long walk in the woods with 2 dogs off leash. They wear e-collars and I have the remote ready, but it’s not used. No real training here, just a demonstration of a relaxed walk.
